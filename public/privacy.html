Privacy Policy

What Montra collects, why, and how you stay in control of it.

Last updated: August 2026

1. Who runs Montra

Montra is a personal finance tracker built and maintained by António Galveia, an independent developer based in Lisbon, Portugal. For anything related to this policy or your data, you can reach out directly at montra.support@gmail.com.

2. What data is collected

Montra only collects what is needed to run your account and show you your own finances:

  • Account data: the email address you register with, and your password (stored only as a secure hash — it will never be stored or seen).
  • Financial entries you type in yourself: transaction dates, amounts, categories, and descriptions; budget limits you set; savings goals you create.

Montra does not connect to your bank, does not read your email, and does not collect any financial data automatically — everything comes from what you manually enter.

3. How your data is used

Your data is used exclusively to provide the service to you: showing your monthly summaries, category breakdowns, budget progress, and savings goals. I do not use your data for advertising, I do not sell it, and I do not share it with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted by Supabase, and the application itself runs on Vercel. Both act as infrastructure providers (data processors) — they host the data on our behalf and do not access it for their own purposes.

5. Cookies

Montra uses a single cookie, strictly to keep you logged in. It contains a signed session token, is marked httpOnly (so it can't be read by page scripts), and is not used for tracking, analytics, or advertising. We don't use any third-party tracking cookies.

6. How long your data is kept

Your account and financial entries are kept for as long as your account exists. If you'd like your account and all associated data permanently deleted, email me and I'll take care of it within a reasonable time.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict how it's processed. To exercise any of these rights, contact montra.support@gmail.com.

8. Security

Passwords are never stored in plain text — they're hashed before being saved. All traffic between your browser and Montra is encrypted (HTTPS). No online service can guarantee perfect security, but Montra takes reasonable steps to protect your data.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, I'll update the date at the top of this page. Continuing to use Montra after a change means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach out at montra.support@gmail.com, or use the contact page.
