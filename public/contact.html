Frequently Asked Questions

Does Montra connect to my bank?

No. Montra doesn't offer bank integrations - you record each income and expense manually, which keeps everything simple and under your control.

Is my data shared?

Montra does not share your financial data with third parties. It is used solely to show you your own summaries and budgets.

Can I use Montra for free?

Yes, creating an account and starting to record transactions is absolutely free.

How do I report an error?

Use the form above with the subject "Report a problem" - describe what happened, and the dev will reply as soon as possible.
