What is Montra

A clear view over your money.

No bank connections or automatic syncing. Montra is simple at its core: you log every transaction yourself, and it shows you what you need to know to make better decisions.

  1. 01

    Record it in your own way

    Add income and expenses manually in seconds—without sharing banking credentials or relying on integrations.

  2. 02

    Each movement in it's category.

    Choose a category when you register, and Montra automatically groups everything into clear monthly summaries.

  3. 03

    Set purposeful budgets.

    Set limits by category and get notified before you exceed them — not afterwards.

Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't.

  • Always up-to-date summary

    Every transaction you record instantly updates your balance and categories—a daily summary you can grasp at a glance.

  • Budgets by category

    Set a monthly limit for housing, leisure, or food and track the progress bar in real time.

  • Warnings before the squeeze

    Montra notifies you when a category is approaching its limit, before the budget gets out of hand.
